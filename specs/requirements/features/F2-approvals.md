# Approvals

## Purpose

Managers review the expense claims submitted by their team and approve or reject each one.

Needs: F1.

## User Stories

- F2.1 As a manager, I see my team's pending claims in one list.
- F2.2 As a manager, I view a claim's full details and its receipt image before deciding.
- F2.3 As a manager, I approve a claim.
- F2.4 As a manager, I reject a claim, giving a reason the employee will see.

## Decisions

- A claim is approved by the employee's direct manager only; there is no delegate or alternate approver.
- Manager approval is final — there is no second approval level, regardless of amount.
- Rejecting a claim always requires a reason.
