# Notifications

## Purpose

Keeps employees and managers informed as a claim moves through submission and approval.

Needs: F1, F2.

## User Stories

- F4.1 As a manager, I get an in-app notification when one of my team submits a claim.
- F4.2 As an employee, I get an in-app notification when my claim is approved or rejected.

## Decisions

- Notifications are in-app only; there is no email delivery.
- Notifications fire on submission and on decision only; there is no reminder for claims pending too long.
